Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What we collect

When you sign in with TikTok, the app receives your TikTok open ID, display name, avatar and the posting options TikTok allows for your account, plus an access token and refresh token.

How we use it

Only to show you which account you are posting to and to upload the videos you choose. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or anything else.

Where it is stored

Tokens and settings are stored only on the computer running the app. They are not sent to any server other than TikTok's, and are never sold or shared.

Deleting your data

Revoke access in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions) and the tokens stop working. Email us and we will delete any stored token and upload log within 30 days.

Children

The app is not meant for anyone under 18.
