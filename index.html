TinyRipple Uploader

TinyRipple Uploader is a desktop tool made by TinyRipple Studio, the creator of short animated science videos on YouTube and TikTok (@tiny.ripple.studio).

It publishes TinyRipple Studio's own finished videos to its TikTok account. The creator signs in with TikTok, reviews each video, writes the caption, picks who can view it and confirms before anything is posted.
